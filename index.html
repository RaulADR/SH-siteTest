Who we are

A team, not a company.

Soft Hoarders — FTC 12560 — started in 2016 in a corner of a physics lab at „Frații Buzești” National College in Craiova. We are students first; the robot comes second.

We still argue about gearbox suppliers. Nine years in, we have not grown out of it.

02

What we run

The robot is the excuse.

Past the competition robot we run a small STEM programme for schools, libraries and anyone curious enough to knock.

  1. 01

    Competition robot

    Every season: design, machine, wire and program one fully autonomous, tele-operated robot.

    • Mechanical design & manufacturing
    • Autonomous software · Java / FTC SDK
    • CAD & 3D-printed custom parts
  2. 02

    Workshops in schools

    Hands-on sessions for primary classes: mechanisms, sensors, a little code.

  3. 03

    Demos & open days

    We take the robot to fairs and libraries and explain what is under the cover.

  4. 04

    Mentoring new members

    Senior students run the onboarding: assembly, Java, CAD, and the parts nobody admits to.

  5. 05

    Outreach, all year

    Fifty-seven outreach events in one season, across Romania — mostly school halls.

Want us at your school or event? We bring the robot and our own cables.

How we work

Three rules we keep breaking

More tendencies than rules. Written on the lab wall in a student's handwriting — two of them have been crossed out.

Self-improvement

We rewrite the same subsystem twice on purpose. The first teaches you; the second survives a match.

02

Innovation in STEM

Curiosity gets the vote. Our best parts started as a “what if we tried…” at 11 p.m.
